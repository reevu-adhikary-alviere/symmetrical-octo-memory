# Cash Loading

Let customers load cash into their wallet by walking into a retail partner location with a barcode. You generate a one-time barcode through the API, the customer presents it at the register, and once they hand over cash the funds appear in their wallet.

Useful for cash-preferred consumers, the underbanked, and any program where physical cash on-ramps matter.
