# Internal Transfers

Move funds between two wallets on the same Alviere program. Internal transfers settle instantly because the money never leaves the Alviere ledger. You're just moving balance from one wallet to another.

Use internal transfers for things like P2P payments between consumers, splitting funds between a consumer's own wallets, or shifting balance between a business account and one of its subsidiaries.
