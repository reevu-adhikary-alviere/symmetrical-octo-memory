# Wire

Move funds by domestic wire transfer, with same-day settlement through the US wire network. Use wires when you need irrevocable settlement during the banking day and the amount justifies the wire fee.

Wire transfer endpoints live in the [V2 API Reference](/api-v2) under **Money Movement**.
